Un Polar Bear-oda letter

Dear Meow,

Innaikku un birthday. Naan un pakkathula illa nu konjam kashtama dhaan irukku. Aana indha chinna page moolama, naan unnoda dhaan irukken.

Un sirippu, un kovam, un "meow", ellaamey enakku romba special. Nee en life-la vandha appuram dhaan, saadharana naal kooda azhaga theriyudhu.

Indha varusham un ella kanavum nadakkanum. Nee eppovum ippadiye sirichikittu irukkanum. Adhukku naan eppovum un pakkathula iruppen, dhooram irundhaalum.

"Naan thedina ellaa kelvikkum,
badhil nee dhaan."

Happy birthday, en Meow.

Un Polar Bear 🐻‍❄️
